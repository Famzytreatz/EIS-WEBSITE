About Us

Eliud International School

At Eliud International School, we operate with the H.O.L.O.N™ model, which represents a Holistic, Organized, Leveraged, Organic, and Nurturing approach to education. This model ensures that every child receives balanced development—intellectually, socially, emotionally, and ethically. Our learning environment is carefully designed to encourage curiosity, discipline, creativity, and confidence. We believe that education goes beyond the classroom, so we focus on character building, competence, creativity, and global awareness, preparing our students to thrive in a rapidly changing world. Located in Port Harcourt, Eliud International School remains dedicated to excellence, innovation, and the continuous growth of every learner entrusted to our care. Eliud International School — Where students grow in character, competence, creativity, and global consciousness.
